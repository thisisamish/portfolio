About

I like understanding how things work, and then making them work better for the people who depend on them.

As a kid I clipped the tech columns out of the Sunday newspaper, hoping to build apps like the ones they described. That curiosity never left. I started with the web and moved steadily deeper into systems, where fundamentals matter and details count.

At work I'm the person people come to when they're stuck. I notice small things, I write things down so others don't have to guess, and I like leaving a codebase easier to work in than I found it.

Outside work I read, watch far too many films, and eat a lot of noodles.
